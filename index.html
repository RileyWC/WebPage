First & Second PC Build (2022 - 2023)

I built my first computer for myself in December 2022 and used it for my assignments for school, TV and Youtube watching, gaming, and occasionally 3D printing. This taught me about the different components behind PC functionality, as well as how to dual boot a system. I dual booted Linux and Windows to separate my school and leisure activities, and learned about USB booting devices like TailsOS in the process. This led to a second PC build for my brother, which gave me more familiarity with building computers and debugging different hardware issues that can occur before booting a system.

Pi-Hole DNS with Ad Blocking and Pi-VPN (2023)

After the RetroPi stopped getting use, I decided to use the Raspberry Pi for something new. I saw some Crosstalk Solutions videod about turning a Pi into a VPN server and an Ad-Blocking homemade DNS server. After following the tutorials and tweaking the settings to my liking, I had a VPN I could use to SSH into my PC for homework away from home, and faster internet thanks to the DNS blocking ad domains. This taught me about more opensource projects, the DNS protocol, VPNs like OpenVPN and Wireguard, and about SSH security.
