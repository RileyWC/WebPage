Author Recognition and Recreation Using Markov Chains (2023)

During my sophomore year in college, I worked on a class project that asked students to learn about markov chains and use them to make a crude program that can mimic the writing style of authors. The approach I implemented involved tokenizing characters and then whole words to predict what the next word is likely to be. The algorithm could look back one, two, or "k" words backwards in order to calculate what a likely next word would be. Keeping "k" as a variable allowed the class to explore how different k values affected the coherency of the models.
